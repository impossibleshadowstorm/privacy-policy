Account Deletion Request

Last updated: October 13, 2026

This page explains how users of Books N Mee can delete their account and associated personal data.

Option 1: Delete in the App (fastest)

Open Books N Mee, go to Settings > Delete Account, and confirm. Your account and data are deleted immediately.

Option 2: Request by Email

If you can't access the App, email rate.code.115@gmail.com using the email address associated with your account.

Use the subject line: Books N Mee Account Deletion Request, and include your registered email address so we can identify your account.

What Data Will Be Deleted?

When your request is processed, we will delete your account and associated personal data, including:

  • Profile information (name, email, username, profile photo)
  • Library data (books, reading sessions, notes, goals, and stats)
  • Uploaded photos (profile and book cover photos)
  • Social content (comments and shared notes)

Data Retention

Data that is not required to be retained for legal, security, or legitimate business purposes will be deleted. If any data must be retained, it will be kept only for the period required by applicable law or other valid retention requirements.

Processing Time

In-app deletion is immediate. Email requests are processed within 30 days.

For assistance, contact rate.code.115@gmail.com.
