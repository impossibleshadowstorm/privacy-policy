Privacy Policy for Books N Mee

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Books N Mee ("the App") is developed and operated by an independent developer. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects and how it is used when you use Books N Mee.

Account Creation

The App requires you to sign in with your Google account (via Google Sign-In / Firebase Authentication) to create an account. When you sign in, we receive your name, email address, and profile photo from Google.

Information We Collect

  • Profile information - your name, email address, and profile photo from Google Sign-In, and any username or bio you set in the App.
  • Library data - books you add to your library, reading sessions, notes, reading goals, and reading statistics.
  • Photos - profile photos and custom book cover photos you upload.
  • Social content - if you use community features, your username, comments, and shared notes may be visible to other users.

This data is stored in our Firebase (Google) backend and is tied to your account.

Book Search

When you search for a book, your search text or scanned ISBN is sent to the Open Library public API (operated by the Internet Archive) to look up book details and cover images. No account information is sent with these requests.

On-Device Processing

Barcode scanning and text recognition (used to look up books by cover or ISBN) are processed on your device and are not uploaded anywhere.

Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services that may collect information used to identify you:

  • Google Sign-In & Firebase Authentication - to create and sign in to your account.
  • Cloud Firestore & Firebase Storage (Google) - to store your library data, notes, and photos.
  • Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, Performance Monitoring, and App Check (Google) - for app analytics, crash reporting, performance diagnostics, and abuse prevention.

These Firebase and Google services are governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Account and Data Deletion

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time directly within the App (Settings > Delete Account). This permanently removes your profile, library, notes, comments, stats, and photos.

You can also request deletion through our account deletion page.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

Data Retention and Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data. We do not sell your personal information.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
