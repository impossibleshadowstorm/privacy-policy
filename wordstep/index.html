Privacy Policy for Wordstep

Last updated: September 15, 2026

Wordstep ("the App") is developed and operated by an independent developer. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects and how it is used when you use Wordstep.

Information We Collect

Wordstep does not require account registration and does not collect personally identifiable information directly from you. Your player name and chosen avatar are stored only on your device and are never transmitted to us. The App may use third-party services that collect information used to identify you, as described below.

Local Data Storage

Your game progress, stats, achievements, and coin balance are stored locally on your device using an on-device database. This data stays on your device and is not uploaded to any server we operate.

Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services that may collect information used to identify you:

  • Firebase (Google) - for app analytics, crash reporting, and performance monitoring. Governed by Firebase's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Mobile Ads - to display rewarded and interstitial ads within the App. Ad providers may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve relevant ads. Governed by Google's Advertising Policy.

Notifications

Wordstep can send local reminder notifications (e.g. daily streak reminders) directly from your device. These notifications are scheduled and delivered entirely on-device and do not involve sending your data to any server.

Log Data

When you use the App, standard log data such as device type, operating system version, and crash logs may be collected automatically to help diagnose and fix issues.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

Data Retention and Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the third-party services listed above. We do not sell your personal information.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
